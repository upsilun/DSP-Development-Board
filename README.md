# DSP-Development-Board
| Phase                                         | Duration | Target Dates             | Key Goals                                                                                                                   |
| :-------------------------------------------- | :------- | :----------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1️⃣ DSP Familiarization & Bench Tests**     | 2 weeks  | **Oct 25 – Nov 7 2025**  | Bring up TI F28335 eval board, run self-test firmware, record “golden” ADC/PWM metrics, confirm JTAG & UART.                |
| **2️⃣ Hardware Design Planning**              | 1 week   | **Nov 8 – Nov 15**       | Finalize schematic block diagram, decide form factor & power strategy, component shortlist (DC/DC, CAN, UART).              |
| **3️⃣ Schematic & PCB Design (Rev-A)**        | 2 weeks  | **Nov 16 – Nov 30**      | Complete schematic, footprint validation, DRC/ERC clean, produce 4-layer stack-up, ready Gerbers.                           |
| **4️⃣ Prototype Fabrication & Assembly**      | 2 weeks  | **Dec 1 – Dec 14**       | Order PCBs, receive & solder (10 units), visual inspection + continuity.                                                    |
| **5️⃣ Board Bring-Up & Self-Test Validation** | 2 weeks  | **Dec 15 – Dec 28**      | Power/clock verification, JTAG connect, flash self-test firmware, measure ADC noise/PWM jitter, compare to eval board data. |
| **6️⃣ Debug & Refinement**                    | 2 weeks  | **Dec 29 – Jan 11 2026** | Resolve layout/power issues, optimize analog section, finalize “PASS/FAIL” self-test firmware.                              |
| **7️⃣ Final Quality Verification & Report**   | 1 week   | **Jan 12 – Jan 19 2026** | Run full automated self-test, log results, finalize bring-up checklist + documentation.                                     |
| **🎯 Target Completion**                      |          | **Before 20 Jan 2026**   | Fully validated Rev-A board with self-test firmware and reference metrics.                                                  |
